# 🎛️ Admin Content Management Guide for Lahra

## How Lahra Will Manage Her Website Content

### 🏠 **Admin Dashboard** (`/admin`)
**Central command center for all website management**

- **Quick Stats**: Total photos, style looks, dining spots, daily moments
- **Content Overview**: See what's published across all categories
- **Quick Actions**: One-click access to common tasks
- **Status Indicators**: Track what content needs attention

---

## 📸 **Photo Management** (Currently Available)

### **Upload Interface** (`/admin/images`)
- **Drag & Drop**: Upload multiple photos at once
- **Category Selection**: Automatically organize by type
- **Instant Preview**: See photos immediately after upload
- **Batch Operations**: Select and manage multiple photos

### **Photo Organization**
- **Categories**: Outfits, Dining, Daily, Snacks, Travel, General
- **Auto-Naming**: System handles file naming and organization
- **Search & Filter**: Find photos by category, date, or content
- **Gallery Management**: Reorder, feature, or archive photos

### **Restaurant Posts** (`/admin/dining`)
- **Restaurant Details**: Name, location, cuisine type, price range
- **Photo Upload**: Food photos, ambiance shots, exterior views
- **Review Writing**: Personal thoughts, recommendations, ratings
- **Menu Highlights**: Favorite dishes, must-try items
- **Visit Information**: Date visited, occasion, companions

### **Dining Content Types**
1. **Full Restaurant Reviews**
   - Multiple photos with captions
   - Detailed review with ratings
   - Recommendation level (Must Visit, Good, Skip)
   
2. **Quick Food Posts**
   - Single photo with brief caption
   - "What I'm eating today" style content
   
3. **Recipe Shares**
   - Home cooking adventures
   - Recipe attempts and results

### **Restaurant Database**
- **Favorites List**: Quick access to go-to spots
- **Wishlist**: Places to try next
- **Location Mapping**: Organize by neighborhood/city
- **Cuisine Categories**: Italian, French, Asian, etc.

## 🔄 **Content Workflow**

### **Typical Daily Workflow for Lahra**

1. **Morning**: 
   - Upload yesterday's photos
   - Write quick daily post about plans
   - Check poll results from previous day

2. **During Day**:
   - Take photos of outfits, food, activities
   - Quick mobile uploads with basic captions
   - Respond to comments and engagement

3. **Evening**:
   - Review and organize day's content
   - Write detailed posts about experiences
   - Plan tomorrow's content
   - Create polls for audience engagement

### **Weekly Content Planning**
- **Monday**: Week planning, outfit prep
- **Tuesday-Thursday**: Daily content, restaurant visits
- **Friday**: Week recap, weekend plans
- **Saturday-Sunday**: Adventure content, relaxed posts
